About Me!

It's late at night and you have a very important exam tomorrow at school. You've known about this exam for a week or two, but you haven't started studying yet (You keep saying, "I'll start studying tomorrow.") Your brain feels foggy from all the stress and you pass out, hitting your head on the desk in front of you. When you wake up, you're in an unfamiliar place. How do you get out?
